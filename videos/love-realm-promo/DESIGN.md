## Style Prompt
Create a warm, dreamy, intimate promo video for a romance interactive fiction product. The look should feel like soft editorial cinema rather than a tech demo. Use gentle depth, creamy highlights, rose-tinted warmth, and elegant pacing. The interface moments should feel real and readable, but still elevated by composition and motion.

## Colors
- `#fbf6f0` Background glow
- `#f1ddd2` Soft rose wash
- `#8a5537` Primary accent
- `#6d3f26` Deep accent
- `#241914` Text / dark anchor

## Typography
- Headlines: `Source Han Serif SC`, `Noto Serif SC`, serif fallback
- Body/UI: `Source Han Sans SC`, `Noto Sans SC`, sans-serif fallback

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use neon colors or cold blue/purple gradients.
- Do not use generic SaaS card-grid layouts as hero visuals.
- Do not use aggressive zooms, glitch transitions, or strobe-like motion.
- Do not show fake UI that does not exist in the project.
- Do not let subtitles cover important UI detail.
